# Evidura — User stories

**Product:** [PRODUCT.md](./PRODUCT.md)


### Sourcing category manager

- As a category manager, I want candidate offerings mapped into our own capability taxonomy, so that I can tell whether two shortlisted products do the same job before I run two separate evaluations.
- As a category manager, I want a viability grade on each vendor with the evidence behind it, so that I can weight a five-year commitment against a seller's likely lifespan.
- As a category manager, I want the contract protections recommended for this vendor's grade, so that legal starts from a position rather than a template.
- As a category manager, I want prior declines searchable with their reasons, so that we do not spend another quarter re-evaluating an offering we rejected eighteen months ago for reasons that still hold.

### Clinical evaluator, physician or nurse informaticist

- As a clinical evaluator, I want the vendor's evidence pre-appraised with the study population and its distance from our population stated plainly, so that my scarce time goes to clinical judgement rather than reading methods sections.
- As a clinical evaluator, I want to see what our prevalence does to the positive predictive value at the vendor's recommended operating point, so that I can say how many false alarms my colleagues will actually receive.
- As a clinical evaluator, I want subgroup performance shown where it exists and flagged where it does not, so that an absence of evidence is visible rather than invisible.
- As a clinical evaluator, I want to record dissent that survives into the decision record, so that a concern I raised is retrievable if the deployment later goes wrong.

### Legal, contracting, and third-party risk officer

- As a contracting lawyer, I want change-of-control, escrow, portability, and transition-assistance clauses proposed with the reasoning tied to this vendor's viability, so that I can defend an unusual demand to a seller who says nobody else asks for it.
- As a third-party risk officer, I want viability re-underwritten on a cadence after signature, so that a deteriorating vendor is a managed exposure rather than a surprise.
- As a third-party risk officer, I want an alert when a vendor under contract is acquired, discontinues a product, or receives a safety communication, listing our exposed contracts and the protections we hold.

### Finance and actuarial analyst

- As a finance analyst, I want total cost of ownership including integration, clinician time, and exit costs, expressed per unit of work, so that the comparison is not a licence-price beauty contest.
- As a finance analyst, I want overlapping incumbent contracts surfaced with their spend, so that consolidation savings enter the business case for the new purchase.

### Evaluation committee chair and compliance officer

- As a committee chair, I want an evaluation blocked from the vote until the regulatory verification, evidence appraisal, and conflict disclosures are complete, so that the committee is never asked to decide on an incomplete file.
- As a compliance officer, I want the lawful basis, agreement, minimisation, and end-of-evaluation data disposition recorded before any patient data moves to a candidate vendor, so that our own diligence does not become our own breach.
- As a compliance officer, I want an undisclosed evaluator relationship to invalidate the affected scores and force a re-vote, so that the conflict rule has a consequence.
